Many people go missing.

Have you ever wondered where those who go missing and are never found end up? I don't mean what happens to them, no, I mean WHERE they end up. I used to live close to a place forgotten by the world. It's not on any map and the journey to get there is as unintuitive as it is pointless as it now lies entirely derelict. Used to be a mining town, way back when. Nothing bad happened to it per se , which is what's unusual about it. The coal didnt run dry and the land had a enough left to give to keep it going well into the 20th century. The odd part is that it simply withered away.

As I said, many people go missing, but "many" doesnt mean the whole town. 

All and all, more people went missing in the area than we have record of people actually living there since they didnt stop after the town was abandoned. The most recent one that I have on record is from about 8 months ago. The following constitutes the bulk of my snooping into all the cases I could. It's not all in chronological order, so forgive me for that, but it should paint a picture of the going-on's in the area.


Incident report no.1

Connor B. went missing shortly after he got admited to the local hospital. According to his family, he stopped eating almost entirely and would have ocassional episodes where he would freeze and become unresponsive. The police report ends here. What the authorities didn't find, however, is Connor's videos. He had been part of a small niche horror community on the dark web where he would upload videos of him trying urban legend challenges. His latest post to date is called "Looking for the black-eyed children".

The video starts off with Connor trying to get his camera to see out of the windshield before giving up and puting it in the backseat to see out one of the windows. As he is driving, he tries to talk to the camera, but due to its awkward placement its almost entirely inaudible. 

After a 2 hour drive, he stops his car by the edge of a forest as he begins to record the outro.

On  the publically available recording, the outro begins at 2:32:51 and goes on as: "Well, didn't find anything this time, but eh.. y'know, you gotta keep trying or you might miss THE one, like last time at the abandoned factory. Hehe we got covered in roaches at the end but it was so worth it for the footage. Anyways, I'll head home now, see you guys tommorow, and next time we should get together again and-" at this point the footage cuts and the audio breaks, leaving the remaining 3 minutes of footage entirely silent as he casually walks back to his car. The events of the video took place about a week before he went missing.

Thankfully, Connor was sloppy when making his account so I managed to get his log in information since he only had 1 email address and password, so I managed to access his google drive, inside which lay the original unedited footage.

The outro begins at the same time, but right after the public version cuts out, Connor spots something in the trees and the footage seems to freeze. Upon further examination, the body of a person can be seen hanging in the treeline. The camera proceeds to freezr for a moment before losing visuals, leaving the rest of the 31 hours of footage only have audio. The audio is almost entirely comprised of a faint electrical buzzing , eccept for spikes at hour 5, 12, 13, 20 and 29 before visuals and audio returns for the end of the recording. Each spike consists of leaves rustling. After hour 5, 12 and 13, the buzzing grows slightly louder before becoming quieter at hours 20 and 29.

The end of the footage seems to not have been edited, as both the publically available as well as Connor's copy are both entirely silent in the end. 


Incident report no.2

The following is a mix of information from the personal account of an ennebriated doctor present at the scene and an incomplete report from the hospital which the missing person was admitted to.

John Doe was an elderly man who lived on a farm most of his life. He went missing approximatley 2 months after he was admitted to a nearby ICU.

He had come in on a monday morning unable to speak with a sickly, wet rasp to his breath. 5 solid masses could be seen in his lungs on the Xray.

After a 12 hour surgery, the medical staff succesfuly extracted 5 teeth from his lungs:

2 mollars, matching the patients missing teeth1 incissor, which seems to belong to a person around 18-24 years old1 canine, belonging to a common dog, roughly labrador in size1 unidentifed tooth, not matching any known records

13 hours after the surgery, screaming could be heard from John Doe's room. Medical staff rushed in to find Mr. Doe with blood dripping from his mouth, holding a pair of pliers and his own dislodged incissor.

Over the next 20 days, the patient's condition continued to deteriorate, as he was caught multiple times trying to steal different items with which he could remove or shatter his teeth. He had succeeded in removing 29 of his 30 remaining teeth after succesfuly stealing the maintanace crew's hammer. Before he could shatter his final tooth he was restrained, screaming and bawling his eyes out.

The following morning, his room was found empty and his trash can filled with a mix of bile, stomach acid, blood and 128 teeth of varrying size.
